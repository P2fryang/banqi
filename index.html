Rulebook

Goal

Leave your opponent with no legal move, usually by capturing all of their pieces. The general is not royal: losing it does not end the game (unless a custom rule says so).

Setup and turns

32 pieces are shuffled face-down on a 4×8 board. On your turn do exactly one of: flip a face-down piece, move one of your face-up pieces, or capture with one. The first player to flip takes the colour of the piece revealed; the other player gets the other colour.

Moving and capturing

A piece moves one square up, down, left or right into an empty square. It captures by moving onto an adjacent face-up enemy piece of equal or lower rank. Only face-up pieces can capture or be captured.

Default exception: the general cannot capture soldiers, and soldiers can capture the general.

Cannon (Taiwanese)

The cannon moves one square like the others. To capture, it travels in a straight line over exactly one piece of any colour, face-up or face-down (the screen), and takes any face-up enemy piece directly behind it. All squares between the cannon and the screen, and between the screen and the target, must be empty. It cannot capture an adjacent piece.

Ending the game

The player to move with no legal move loses. Repeating positions is not detected, so use Resign to end a stalemate loop.

Free play

No rules are enforced. Click a face-down piece to flip it, or click a face-up piece and then any square to move or capture. Click the same piece again to flip it back.

Custom rules

Choose Custom and edit the JSON:

  • counts: pieces per side (G general, A advisor, E elephant, R chariot, H horse, C cannon, S soldier). Total must be 32 or fewer.
  • rank: higher beats lower, equal can capture equal. For the cannon this is how well it defends.
  • beats: exceptions such as "G>S": false (general cannot take soldier).
  • cannonJumps: true for the jump-capture cannon, false for a plain ranked piece.
  • endOnGeneral: true ends the game when a general is captured.
